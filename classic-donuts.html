Classic Donuts 🍩

A timeless soft and fluffy donut recipe crafted with a premium bakery touch.

Ingredients

  • All purpose flour - 500g
  • Sugar - 60g
  • Yeast - 10g
  • Milk - 250ml
  • Egg - 1 (optional)
  • Butter - 50g
  • Salt - 5g
  • Vanilla essence - 5ml

Method

1. Mix flour, sugar, yeast and salt.
2. Add milk and knead into a soft dough.
3. Add butter and knead until smooth.
4. Rest dough until double in size.
5. Roll and cut donut shapes.
6. Proof again and fry until golden.
7. Finish with chocolate glaze or sugar coating.

Chef Tips

• Keep dough soft for fluffy donuts.
• Do not over fry; maintain golden colour.
• Serve fresh for the best texture.
